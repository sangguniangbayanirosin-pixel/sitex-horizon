Article I — Title and Nature

This Agreement shall be known as the Sitex Horizon Pilot Implementation Agreement. It is a binding memorandum of agreement under Articles 1305, 1306, and 1315 of the Civil Code. Nothing herein shall be construed as a joint venture, partnership, agency to sell franchises, or employment contract, except as expressly stated. The FIRST PARTY remains the operator of the terminal premises; the SECOND PARTY remains solely responsible for its CPCs, vehicles, and crews; the THIRD PARTY remains the author of the System.

Article II — Definition of Terms

For purposes of this Agreement:

“System” means Sitex Horizon, including the public Terminal board, Passenger view, Driver / conductor application, Admin announcement desk, advertisement slot, and Report desk, as described in Annex “A”.

“SITEX” means the Sorsogon Integrated Terminal Exchange, Barangay Balogo, Sorsogon City, adjacent to SM City Sorsogon.

“Live Fix” means a time-stamped latitude, longitude, and speed reading obtained from the GNSS / GPS of the on-duty driver’s or conductor’s smartphone, with the bus plate as the public identifier.

“ETA” means estimated time of arrival at SITEX, computed as remaining official road kilometers of the assigned municipality divided by the current speed in kilometers per hour, continuously recomputed when speed changes.

“On duty” means the interval from the crew’s “Start trip” until “End trip” on the Driver application, and in no case after the crew has been signed off or the bus has been grounded.

“Personal data” has the meaning in Section 3 of R.A. 10173. The public board shall display the bus plate, route, operator name, speed class, and ETA — not the personal name, photograph, phone number, or home address of any driver or conductor.

Article III — Purpose and Scope

The purpose of this Agreement is to:

a. Provide waiting passengers at SITEX a public, free-of-charge view of whether a bus for their municipality is coming, how far it is, and when it is expected, covering the sixteen (16) municipalities listed in Annex “C”;

b. Allow Sitex / SM Sorsogon to publish live transport announcements (including typhoon, LGU travel bans, and limited-bus operations) on a large public board, with emergency hotlines when required;

c. Give Operators a channel for passenger experience reports, which Sitex shall forward to the concerned cooperative or corporation head;

d. Pilot the System first on the routes in Annex “F”, then expand by written addendum;

e. Operate only as a passenger-information and terminal-management aid. It does not replace LTFRB timetables, CPCs, fare matrices, or traffic-enforcement powers of the LTO, PNP, or LGU.

Article V — Obligations of the First Party (Sitex / SM Sorsogon)

The FIRST PARTY shall:

a. Designate SITEX as the official venue of the public board, and provide at least one (1) powered display with internet (wifi or LTE) at a waiting area visible to passengers;

b. Appoint a Sitex dispatcher / administrator who alone may publish announcements, weather status, and advertisement slots, and who shall forward passenger reports to the concerned Operator head within one (1) operational day;

c. Keep Admin credentials confidential. The public URL of the Terminal and Passenger views may be posted; the Admin function shall not be left open to the general public;

d. Honor LGU ordinances, PAGASA signals, and PNP / CDRRMO guidance in announcements, using the System’s Critical / Caution / Info levels, including the public message INGAT PO ANG LAHAT and hotlines when travel is suspended or limited;

e. Not charge waiting passengers any fee to view the board;

f. Act as personal information controller (PIC), jointly with the SECOND PARTY as to crew location data, for processing done on SITEX premises, and maintain a contact point for data-subject requests under Section 16 of R.A. 10173;

g. Cause this Agreement and the privacy notice (Annex “B”) to be posted at the information desk.

Article VI — Obligations of the Second Party (Operators)

Each Operator shall:

a. Enroll the plates that will participate, matching the CPC / authorized units using SITEX;

b. Brief drivers and conductors that the Driver application uses the phone’s built-in GNSS / GPS (not a separate “GPS number”), during an on-duty trip only, and that speed from that fix updates the public ETA;

c. Obtain from each participating crew member a signed Informed Consent in the form of Annex “B” before the first Live Fix, in Filipino or English, as the crew prefers. Consent shall be freely given, specific, informed, and evidenced in writing or recorded electronic means, pursuant to Section 3(b) of R.A. 10173 and NPC Circular No. 2023-04;

d. Instruct crews: Start trip before leaving the origin or SITEX bay; Share live GPS while on duty; End trip upon arrival or grounding; keep mobile data on; never share GPS off duty or from home;

e. Not retaliate against a crew member who withdraws consent; the unit may then be shown as “no live GPS” rather than a false ETA;

f. Receive and act on passenger reports forwarded by Sitex, and name an official contact for that purpose;

g. Remain solely liable for the safe operation of its units, fares, crews, and franchise conditions. The System is information only.

Article VII — Obligations of the Third Party (Proponent)

The THIRD PARTY shall:

a. Grant the FIRST PARTY a royalty-free, non-exclusive, non-transferable license to use the System at SITEX for the Term, for public passenger information and terminal administration, consistent with the Proponent’s offer that the System is given for the people of Sorsogon;

b. Retain intellectual-property ownership of Sitex Horizon (software, design, documentation), except for SITEX / SM marks, which remain with their owners and are used only with permission for identification of the terminal;

c. Assist in training Sitex staff and pilot crews, and in correcting ETA error against measured arrivals during the Pilot;

e. Cooperate in any NPC inquiry and in implementing security measures under Section 20 of R.A. 10173;

f. This license does not make the Proponent the employer of any driver, nor the concessionaire of SITEX.

Article VIII — Data Privacy, GPS, and Proportionality

Section 1. Lawful criteria. Processing of a Live Fix is based on:

(i) the freely given, specific, informed, and recorded consent of the driver / conductor (R.A. 10173, Sec. 12(a); NPC Circular No. 2023-04); and/or

(ii) necessity for the performance of a contract to which the crew or Operator is party, namely the on-duty provision of a public transport service with passenger information (Sec. 12(b)); and

(iii) legitimate passenger-safety and terminal-management purpose compatible with E.O. 202, Sec. 5(k), and R.A. 7394’s right to information — provided that processing remains proportionate (Sec. 11).

Section 2. What is collected. During an on-duty trip only: latitude, longitude, speed, heading if available, accuracy, timestamp, bus plate, route / municipality, and operator name. Not collected: government ID numbers, home address, off-duty location, passenger names, payment data, or continuous audio/video of the cab.

Section 3. What the public sees. Plate, destination, municipality and ZIP, official road kilometers, live or last speed, ETA, and status (en route / delayed / arrived / suspended). The crew’s personal name shall not appear on the public board.

Section 4. Duration. Consent is time-bound to the Term of this Agreement and to each on-duty trip. A Live Fix older than thirty (30) minutes without refresh shall not be presented as current. Raw coordinates shall not be retained longer than ninety (90) days unless a lawful investigation or NPC / court process requires longer holding, after which they shall be deleted or anonymized.

Section 5. Withdrawal and rights. The data subject may withdraw consent, access, correct, or object under Section 16 of R.A. 10173 through the Operator and the Sitex privacy contact. Withdrawal shall not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

Section 6. Security and sharing. Access to Admin and raw telemetry is restricted to Sitex dispatch and the concerned Operator. There shall be no sale of personal data. Sharing with PNP, CDRRMO, or LTFRB shall be only upon lawful request or in a genuine emergency to protect vital interests (Sec. 12(c)).

Section 7. Personal phones (BYOD). Where the crew uses a personal smartphone, participation is voluntary, requires Annex “B”, and shall not extend to personal photos, messages, or off-duty movement. The Operator shall, where practicable, prefer a unit-assigned device.

Section 8. Passenger reports. Reports are operational complaints (category, route, short note, ticket number). The Parties shall not require a passenger’s real name. Free-text notes shall be limited and used only to improve service.

Article IX — Public Announcements and Emergencies

Only the FIRST PARTY (or a person it designates in writing) may publish to all screens. Critical notices (typhoon, LGU ordinance of no travel, grounded fleet) shall occupy the large announcement area and include hotlines of PNP, CDRRMO, Bureau of Fire Protection, and Sitex. The Parties acknowledge that during disasters, R.A. 10121 prefers timely public warning over commercial messages; advertisements shall yield to Critical notices.

Article X — Advertisements

The FIRST PARTY may sell or grant advertisement slots on the board, provided creatives are lawful, not contrary to morals or public policy (Civil Code Art. 1306), and are paused during Critical notices. Revenue, if any, belongs to the FIRST PARTY unless the Parties execute a separate revenue-share addendum. The THIRD PARTY claims no advertisement revenue under this royalty-free license.

Article XI — Pilot, Accuracy, and No Warranty of Exact Minute

The System computes ETA from official road distance and the last known speed of that plate. Traffic, weather, detours, and GPS error may cause variance. The Parties shall, during the first sixty (60) days of the Pilot (Annex “F”), compare board ETA with actual bay arrival and adjust road-kilometer tables if a route is systematically early or late. The board shall not be represented as an LTFRB-official timetable. No Party warrants a particular minute of arrival.

Article XII — Intellectual Property, Marks, and Further Development

Sitex Horizon source and design remain with the THIRD PARTY. SM / SITEX names and the terminal photograph are used only to identify the actual place of public viewing, with the FIRST PARTY’s permission. The THIRD PARTY may use anonymized operational statistics for the MBA thesis at SLMCS, without identifying any crew member. Further development after the Pilot may be agreed in writing.

Article XIII — Term, Renewal, and Termination

This Agreement shall have a term of two (2) years from the date of notarization (the “Term”), unless sooner terminated. It shall automatically renew for successive one-year periods unless a Party gives thirty (30) days’ written notice. Any Party may terminate for material breach remaining uncured after fifteen (15) days’ notice, or immediately if continued GPS processing would violate R.A. 10173. Upon termination, Live Fixes shall cease; public archives older than the retention period shall be deleted; and the license to use the System at SITEX shall end, without prejudice to the FIRST PARTY’s right to return to printed schedules.

Article XIV — Liability and Indemnity

Each Operator remains solely responsible for accidents, CPC violations, and labor claims involving its units and crews. The FIRST PARTY remains responsible for the terminal premises. The THIRD PARTY is responsible for software defects it does not cure within a reasonable time after written notice. No Party shall be liable to another for indirect or consequential damages. Nothing herein waives liability for fraud, willful misconduct, or violation of R.A. 10173. Indemnity is only to the extent of the indemnifying Party’s own fault.

Article XV — Force Majeure

Failure to perform due to typhoon, flood, earthquake, epidemic, internet-backbone failure, or government prohibition shall not be a breach for the duration of the event. The FIRST PARTY shall nonetheless endeavor to publish a Critical notice of no or limited travel.

Article XVI — Dispute Resolution and Venue

The Parties shall first attempt amicable settlement, then mediation at the City of Sorsogon. If unresolved, exclusive venue shall be the proper courts of Sorsogon City, Province of Sorsogon, to the exclusion of all others. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of the Philippines.

Annex “A”

Description of the Sitex Horizon System

Sitex Horizon is a web-based public information system for SITEX. Screens: (1) Terminal — glassmorphic public board over an HD view of the SITEX building, live map of Sorsogon, next arrivals, announcement, advertisements; (2) Passenger — choose a municipality, see the next bus and ETA; (3) Driver / conductor — select plate, start/end trip, share live GPS, optional speed override for demonstration; (4) Admin — announcements, weather, ads, report forwarding; (5) Report — anonymous operational complaint with a ticket number.

Accuracy method. The System does not use a “GPS number.” It uses the phone’s built-in GNSS (Android and iOS Geolocation: latitude, longitude, coords.speed). Remaining official road kilometers for that municipality from SITEX, divided by current speed, equals ETA in minutes. When velocity of that particular bus number changes, ETA is recomputed. Official road distances are those in Annex “C”.

Internet connectivity is required for a Live Fix on a driver phone to appear on the Sitex TV and on other passenger phones. Without internet the last local view may remain, labeled as not live.
